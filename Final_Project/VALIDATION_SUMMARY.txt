PROJECT VALIDATION AND VIDEO UPDATE — 5 November 2026

CURRENT REPORT AND EXPERIMENT
The main report has 12 pages. Two earlier detailed result pages are retained
as Supporting_Results.pdf. The report and new figures were visually reviewed.
The full new experiment ran in MATLAB R2026a Update 4 on 4 November Sydney time:
216 new inputs plus 36 old diagnostic inputs, six methods and 1,512 method rows.
New sentences belong to the same six known speakers. The default and matched
SPP smoothers were frozen before scoring; no result-based selection was made.

NUMERICAL CHECKS
All old 36-by-4 baseline waveforms are bit-identical to archived outputs.
Independent Python scoring checks all SNR, late SNR, noise-update recurrences,
effective weights and tracking diagnostics; all main STOI values use standard
PySTOI 0.4.1, extended=False, on unquantized doubles. An independent audit also
recomputes all sentence, speaker and paired summaries, and representative STOI.
See spp_recovery/ANALYSIS_SUMMARY.json and spp_recovery/checks/ for exact errors.
The quick demo separately recomputes the preselected new example from WAVs and
checks its fresh SNR and tracking measures. Its STOI values are labelled stored.

INTERPRETATION
SPP improves rising-noise tracking and reference SNR but costs mean STOI.
No universal enhancement benefit, subjective listening result or unseen-speaker
generalisation is claimed. All outcomes, including constant-noise costs, remain.
