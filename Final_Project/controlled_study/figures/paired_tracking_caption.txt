Figure 4. Matched noise tracking for the original diagnostic sentence sp02 with street noise: (a) +6 dB rise and (b) -6 dB fall. Gray traces show the actual injected-noise broadband power in each 25 ms analysis window; estimator traces show the summed noise PSD before each update. All powers are normalized by the same initial estimate. The 50 ms ramp is shaded; the dotted line marks the late interval, which begins 100 ms after the ramp. Raster marks identify accepted noise updates and the independent low-clean-energy proxy, not manually labelled silence. Across 109 late frames per condition, neither gate updates after the rise; the original and selected gates accept 31 and 11 updates after the fall. These paired traces illustrate the asymmetry of gating, not a general guarantee of tracking accuracy or perceptual improvement.
